# Code review after M9, 2026-10-24

This review covers the whole code base at commit 6ab1550, the close of M9. It looks at correctness, memory, allocations, and the match between the documents and the code. It also says what M10 needs before it can start.

Eight reviewers read one area each. The areas are the render engine with the ink path, text with cards and previews, `CanvasFeature`, and `Persistence`. The other four are the Tier A packages without `Persistence`, the four small packages, the app target, and the tools with the documents. The four small packages are `Export`, `WorkspaceFeature`, `BackupFeature`, and `DesignSystem`. Each finding below has a status:

- Fixed: the fix is on branch `review-m9`, with a test that fails before the fix, or with a measure.
- Rejected: a benchmark showed no gain.
- Open: read in the code, not fixed. The section gives the reason.

Device measures come from the iPad Pro 11-inch (M4), in Release. Mac measures come from an Apple M4 Pro, in release builds. Each device run has a result bundle under `.build/Runs` with the prefix `V-`. The scripts of the Mac measures are in the scratchpad of the review session and are not part of the repository.

The product owner decided D-168 to D-171 during the review.

## 1. Fixed

### 1.1 A restore on a read-only library stopped the app (D-168)

A library of a newer schema opens read-only, and the selector still offers "Restore from a snapshot". The flow checked the file, saved, and wrote the pre-restore snapshot. Then the release precondition `!isReadOnly` of `SQLiteLibraryRepository.restore(from:stop:)` stopped the app. The product owner chose to allow the restore. The restore now drops the read-only connection and copies with a connection that writes. `RestoreEdgeTests.readOnlyLibraryTakesRestore` stopped at the precondition with the old code.

### 1.2 Input during a restore or "Back up now" (D-169)

The selector and the canvas stayed live from the save of D-149 to the copy. Three faults came from it:

- A stroke after the save stayed pending, and the release precondition `pending.isEmpty` of `restore` stopped the app. A probe on the Mac stopped at "A restore drops 1 pending rows".
- A second restore during the first one replaced the new composition without a close. The library, the checkpointer, and the device state of that composition stayed open.
- A workspace that the selector made during the pre-restore snapshot was in neither file.

`AppComposition.beginLibraryWork()` now turns off the input of the container view and the edits of the canvas. Undo and redo from the keyboard and the card button respect the edit flag too. Before, they ignored it, also on a read-only library, where a new card showed and its row was lost with a log line only. A second restore gives `.ignored`, and a second "Back up now" gives `nil`. `LibraryBusyTests` and `CanvasHistoryTests.noEditsWhileBusy` cover it.

### 1.3 Values that pass the finite checks of a load (D-170)

If its values are finite, a damaged row or a restored snapshot passes the load. Three such values stopped the app, measured on the Mac:

- Strokes at x = -1e308 and x = 1e308 gave an infinite page width, and the release precondition of `PDFExporter.pageLayout` stopped the export.
- A `lastTimestamp` of (UInt64.max, UInt32.max) overflowed at the first `now()` of the clock.
- A card frame of 32,760 by 32,760 points touches 4,096 cells. The renderer adds the title and a margin, 4,356 cells, and the release precondition of `SpatialGrid.insert` stopped the app at each open of that canvas.

The load now refuses an item past `Limits.maxCanvasCoordinate`, checks a card frame with `Limits.cardTitleReach`, and refuses a clock time past `UUIDVersion7.maxMillis`. The camera center stays inside `Limits.maxCameraCoordinate`, so the pen and a drag cannot reach the bound. The clock never passes 48 bits. `ItemBoundTests`, `ValueCodecTests`, `HybridClockTests`, and `CameraControllerTests` failed with the old code.

### 1.4 A failed "Back up now" showed the restore message (D-171)

`BackupMessage(_:)` mapped each other error to `restoreStopped`, so a failed `VACUUM INTO` showed "The restore did not finish". `BackupMessage.backUpFailed` and `BackupMessage(backUp:)` replace it, with a new reference image. A failed delete of an old snapshot after a good write threw too, and the flow showed a failure while the new snapshot existed. Both writers now log that delete and return the snapshot (`RestoreEdgeTests.lockedOldDailySnapshot`, `lockedPreRestoreSnapshot`).

### 1.5 A restore reported "stopped" after the copy

When the `TRUNCATE` checkpoint after the copy failed, `restore` threw. A reader holds it for the 2 s of `busy_timeout`. The app then showed the message of a stop, but the library held the snapshot. The copy is complete in the WAL, so the restore now logs the checkpoint and returns. `RestoreEdgeTests.readerAfterCopy` threw "database is locked" after 2.24 s with the old code.

### 1.6 The frozen parent of an open drew at 120 Hz

`FrameRenderer.needsMoreFrames` took its branch "no committed layer yet" after the freeze of an open, which frees the layer. The parent thus drew a frame at each update of the display link until the swap, also after the flight. The rule now asks only for the freeze and the previews during an open. `TransitionTests.frozenParentRests` covers it. In the full device run, the open of a card took 44 frames to the child instead of 56, with 0 late frames. These are the frames of the parent that stopped after the flight.

### 1.7 A pinch sample after the start of a card flight

A flight sets the pinch of `CameraController` to `nil`. A `.changed` sample of a running pinch then reached the release precondition "No pinch to update", and a pan sample stopped the flight. The canvas now ignores the pan and the start and the samples of a pinch during a flight, and `updatePinch` does nothing without a pinch. `CameraControllerTests.pinchAfterFlight` stopped at the precondition with the old code.

### 1.8 The lifetimes around a restore

- The expiry of the extra time set only the stop flag. The task ended after the next step of 4 MB, but iOS stops an app that does not end its task in the handler. `ExtraTime` ends the task inside its handler. Each final save has its own grant too. The end of an earlier save thus no longer ends a later one (review of 2026-10-24, section 3.2, item 3). `LibraryBusyTests.expiryEndsTheGrant` and `eachSaveEndsItsOwnTime` cover both.
- The preview cache went after the copy. A stop of the process between the two left the previews of the old library. The cache now goes before the copy.
- `SceneDelegate` swapped the root without a dismiss of its presentations, so an alert or a sheet kept the old composition. `AppViewController.install(_:in:)` dismisses them first. `LibraryBusyTests.oldCompositionGoes` shows the old composition, its container, its canvas, and its renderer go after a restore with an open alert.
- After the way back of a failed restore, the scene delegate started the idle timer on the replaced composition. That composition then went away, and its timer stayed on the run loop. A closed composition now starts no timer.
- `share(_:at:)` set `isSharing` before the present. If UIKit refused the present, for example above the palette popover, the flag stayed, and no daily snapshot ran for the rest of the run. The sheet now shows above the top presentation, and the flag follows its presentation.
- `SnapshotPicker` kept itself until a pick or a cancel. The view now keeps its delegate, so both go together. `BackupFeatureTests.pickerGoesWithItsView` failed with the old code.

### 1.9 Previews

- The offscreen preview read the readback buffer without a check of the command buffer. If the GPU refused the work, for example in the background, the cache got a black preview. The render now throws, and the app logs the error.
- A `PreviewLoader` whose canvas went away served all queued requests, up to 16 loads of whole child canvases. It now stops before the next request. `PreviewTests.goneLoaderStops` counts the calls of a source that waits.
- The offscreen preview drew a card whose frame touched the region, and no other card. The title lies above the frame, so a title in the region was missing. The filter now widens the region by `Limits.cardTitleReach`.

### 1.10 Smaller fixes

- The tie of two text boxes with equal sort keys followed the order of a `Dictionary`. The ID now breaks it, as for cards, and `card(at:)` compares IDs without two `uuidString` values (review of 2026-10-24, section 3.1, item 3). Equal keys are reachable: the fallback of `nextTextSortKey` and `nextCardSortKey` gives the last key again.
- `endTextEdit` resigned the first responder and gave it to nobody, so undo and redo from a hardware keyboard stopped after the first edit. The canvas takes it back (`TextEditEdgeTests.keyCommandsAfterEdit`).
- `PencilTouchObserver.touchesMoved` looped over the bridged `Set<UITouch>`, which makes an iterator object at each sample. Measured on the Mac with an `NSObject` set through an `@objc` call: 1,000 allocations per 1,000 calls with the old loop, and 0 with the loop over the `NSSet` (PERF-01).
- `FractionalIndex.init(rawValue:)` copied the bytes of each key into an array. Measured on the Mac, a load of 5,000 cards made 5,016 allocations before and 16 after. A load of 5,000 text boxes of 200 characters made 10,016 before and 5,016 after. The times stayed within the noise of 4.1 to 4.4 ms.

### 1.11 Documents

- README: the status after M9, and the reviews in the list of documents.
- decisions.md: the rows of D-166 and D-167 in the summary, and D-168 to D-171. D-167 states the sum by buckets, about 59 MB.
- architecture.md: the fill of a card, five actors that touch SQLite, and the bound of a grid query. Also `FrameLoopTests` without a limit, `ExtraTime`, and the new limits in section 10.
- data-model.md: the case of the preview file names, and the clock after a restore and at 48 bits. Also the rebalance that is not built, the move of a card inside its canvas, and the place of `CanvasSessions`.
- storage.md: the code that runs SQL, the conflict clauses, and the restore of a read-only library. Also the input during a restore, the checkpoint after the copy, and the time of the cache clear.

## 2. Rejected

### 2.1 `SQLITE_STATIC` for the point blob

A save of 240 strokes of 300 points, 50 saves, 3 alternating runs on the Mac: 3.34 to 3.59 ms with `SQLITE_TRANSIENT` and 3.14 to 3.80 ms with `SQLITE_STATIC`. The bind makes 240 fewer allocations per save, but the time difference is inside the noise.

### 2.2 `journal_size_limit`

After one save of 20,000 strokes, the `-wal` file held 95 MB. After the next checkpoint and save, it held 0 bytes without the pragma, and 4 MB with a limit of 4 MB. SQLite on Apple platforms already truncates the WAL.

### 2.3 One text layout in the PDF (review of 2026-10-24, section 3.3, item 8)

A model of both paths with the same Core Text calls on the Mac: 5,000 boxes take 0.378 to 0.399 s with two layouts and 0.320 to 0.329 s with one. One layout must keep all lines until the page size is known: 105 MB of peak memory against 17 MB for 10,000 boxes. The gain is under 1 % of a D-56 export of about 7 s. The code stays.

### 2.4 The copy of `PreviewDecoder` (review of 2026-10-24, section 3.3, item 7)

A decode at 512 pixels took 302 to 323 µs with the copy and 303 to 306 µs straight into a new array. The time stays. The 256 KB buffer of each loader brings no gain, so M10 can drop it.

## 3. Open findings

Each item names the place, the fault, and the reason why it is not fixed here.

### 3.1 Needs a decision of the product owner

1. M10 acceptance 1 compares each performance test with its baseline, but no stored baseline exists: no `.xcbaseline` file, no test plan, and no compare script. `RenderingPerformanceTests`, the `measure` blocks of `EraseStepPerformanceTests`, `PreviewMakerPerformanceTests`, and `PreviewsAfterRestoreTests` assert nothing. Seven of the 16 budgets of architecture.md section 12 have no automated check: pencil to ink, the pen frame, dropped frames, the save, and the three rows of M9 without a budget. The 10 % compare of the checklist runs by hand in the plan documents. The product owner must say what a baseline is before M10 can meet its acceptance 1.
2. CONTRACT-02 counts each `guard` that returns, also `return []`, and `Scripts/assertion-density.swift` counts the checks of a nested function in the outer function too. The nested function `result` of `PreviewLoader.load` has 8 lines, so the fix alone puts Rendering at 1.996. If only `throw`, `return nil`, `return false`, and `.failure` count, Model falls to 1.98, InkEngine to 1.89, Persistence to 1.97, and Rendering to 1.95. About 75 checks restate the line before them (listed by file and line in the review notes of section 4). The product owner must say which exits count before M10 removes weak checks.
3. code-of-code.md names enforcement that does not exist: FLOW-04 (nesting depth) and SAFE-03 have no lint rule, SCOPE-01 has no dead-code check, and TEST-02 checks only the percentages, not one test per function. AGENT-03 forbids a change of the standard without the product owner.
4. D-132 is open: the capture warnings in the Metal handlers do not become errors.
5. A restore does not survive a stop of the process. Suppose that the restored library does not open, and the process ends before the way back. The next launch then shows "libraryFailed" without the selector, so no restore command is reachable. The pre-restore snapshot exists, but the app cannot use it.
6. The grid budget per canvas of D-170 needs a refusal at the pen. That refusal needs the message of the stroke limit (review of 2026-10-24, section 3.1, item 4).
7. The rebalance of sort keys of data-model.md section 6 is not built. A canvas gets equal keys after about 3,874 appends at one end (measured on the Mac).
8. The undo histories hold at most 16,000,000 points, but the history of the shown canvas is outside `CanvasSessions` (review of 2026-10-24, section 3.1, item 5). The budget counts points, not bytes: measured on the Mac, 4,000,000 points take 67 MB as strokes of 300 points, and 433 MB as strokes of 2 points.

### 3.2 Correctness, fix with a test in M10

1. `RenderEngine.isLoadingText`, `isLoadingCards`, and `isLoadingPreviews` can end a wait early. `take()` moves the text and the cards out of the inbox before the frame applies them. The flags are then false for up to one frame while work waits. Already in the M10 list.
2. The completions of a preview render run under a lock. The inbox lock holds at `CanvasRenderer+Cards.swift:37`. The frame lock holds at `FrameRenderer+PreviewRender.swift:17` and `:99`, and at `PreviewRenderer.swift:117` and `:120`. A completion that posts to the same engine locks twice and stops the app. Already in the M10 list.
3. `giveParentImage` takes the frame lock of two engines on the main actor. The render thread can hold that lock across a GPU wait of `DrawListRing.beginFrame` (review of 2026-10-24, section 3.3, item 3).
4. Three items of the review of 2026-10-24, section 3.2, are unchanged. They are the camera semaphore (item 4), the frozen frame without `markUsed` (item 6), and the silent drop of `PreviewAtlas.want` past 16 requests (item 5).
5. A preview that fails to render gives `.unavailable`. The card then does not ask again for the rest of the session, also after a short failure. It must give `.dropped`.
6. At zoom 0.25 over D-56, about 2,000 cards can need a preview against 512 slots. `PreviewSlots.allocate` evicts the oldest slot of a card that the frame did not draw. The loads thus can fail to come to rest. No test checks that the frame loop of that case goes idle.
7. A failed `CommittedLayer` init retries and logs at each frame.
8. `RowChannel` has no `deinit`, so a canvas that goes without `willMove(toParent:)` keeps its consumer task and the library. A send after `finish()` drops rows with `assertionFailure` only (review of 2026-10-24, section 3.4, item 4).
9. The close of a canvas does not cancel its export (`CanvasViewController+Export.swift:26`). The detached task is not a child task, and `PDFExporter` never checks for a cancel. An export of 200,000 strokes keeps about 1 GB of points for up to 17 s.
10. `WorkspaceSelectorModel.rename` and `create` use an index and a sort key from before an `await` (review of 2026-10-24, section 3.2, item 7).
11. Three items of the review of 2026-10-24, section 3.2, are unchanged. The launch does not ask for a checkpoint after its cleanup (item 8). `RowReader.double()` reads before the `NULL` check (item 10). `PreviewStore.write` reads `errno` late (item 11). The busy path of the hard-limit checkpoint has no test: the test hook blocks before the checkpoint lock.
12. The PDF writes text boxes in the order of a `Dictionary`, which changes per launch. Two exports can never give equal bytes, because Core Graphics writes the dates and a random `/ID`. The real effect is the order of copy, search, and VoiceOver in Preview. A sort by position and ID took 5.6 to 6.0 ms for 10,000 boxes on the Mac.
13. `PreviewFiles` keeps the view size of its creation, so after a rotation the offscreen region differs from the region of the screen.
14. A user string holds numbers that copy limits: `WorkspaceStrings.swift:19` ("1,000") and `:24` ("1 to 120").

### 3.3 Memory and allocations, measure in M10

1. `RenderInbox.reset()` drops the capacity of the edit lists: 1 allocation per post after each frame, 0 with kept lists (Mac). A card drag and a text-box drag add 1 for the array of the moved item. Already in the M10 list.
2. `CanvasRenderer.deliver(upTo:)` copies its array at each delivery: 1 allocation, 0 with an index loop (Mac). Already in the M10 list.
3. Each `CanvasRenderer` builds its own `InkResources`. On the Mac, the first set took 122 ms cold and 2.1 ms warm, and each later set 0.09 to 0.26 ms. Each renderer reserves 26.2 MB of Metal memory, also for an empty card. The arena takes 16 MB, not the 8 MB that `RendererConfiguration.initialArenaSlabs` states, because a buffer always holds 128 slabs.
4. `PreviewMaker` keeps a whole `FrameRenderer` for the life of the composition. It holds an unused arena of 16 MB, a glyph table of 1.31 MB, and 960 KB of text lists. Each offscreen preview builds a new `GlyphAtlas`, `GlyphRing`, and `TextEncoder`. The arena of each pass is sized for the worst case: 5.9 MB for 2,000 strokes of 60 points that need 1.0 MB. A Mac measure showed that one GPU read makes the whole shared buffer resident. The text filter `mayTouch` passes about 2.6 times the boxes that touch the region.
5. The lists of `CommittedLayer` start empty with each new layer, after a rotation and after each leave. On the Mac, growth from 4,096 to 200,000 entries took 6 allocations and about 0.4 ms. It runs inside a frame (review of 2026-10-24, section 3.3, item 4).
6. `GlyphAtlas.insert` copies the key list of a page for each new glyph: 4,000 inserts took 807 µs against 24 µs in place. `TextEncoder` looks up the font ID of the base font for each box: 0.43 ms per 5,000 boxes per pass, 0.046 ms with an identity check (items 5 and 6 of the review of 2026-10-24).
7. The release preconditions of `Eraser.command` and `EraseStrokes.init` build two sets per erase. On the Mac, they take 12 to 17 % of `Eraser.command`: 0.17 ms at 1,000 candidates and 1.3 ms at 20,000. Both sets are unique by construction. Already in the M10 list.
8. The parallel split of `Eraser` costs 0.38 to 0.52 µs per candidate whose bounds miss the path, against 0.12 µs on one thread. 19,000 misses take 7 to 9.8 ms with 8 chunks and 2.3 ms on one thread. A serial prefilter of the bounds takes 0.025 ms.
9. `SpatialGrid.removeEntry` scans a cell: 5.83 ms for the 1,000 oldest of 20,000 strokes in one cell, and 0.09 ms for handwriting (review of 2026-10-24, section 3.3, item 10).
10. `PointArena.allocate` begins a signpost interval with a string at each growth, and the presented handler of a handoff makes a closure in each handoff frame.
11. `History.renderDelta` builds the undo and the redo delta and keeps one. `nextTextSortKey` and `nextCardSortKey` map all keys into an array to take the largest.
12. `CanvasContent` has no reserve at the load: 15 allocations and about 1 ms more for 20,000 strokes on the Mac.

### 3.4 Tools, tests, and rules

1. No repeatable run with Metal API validation. M9 ran it once by hand. Under validation, `FrameAllocationTests` and both `TransitionAllocationTests` fail, because the bare frame makes 121 allocations, so a validation target must skip them.
2. No UI test target exists, but M10 asks for UI tests of the main flows. No control has an `accessibilityIdentifier`, and the rename and the delete of a workspace are reachable only through a context menu.
3. Accessibility for M10 has three gaps. The checkmark of a workspace row reads as "Checkmark". The rename and the delete of a row need `accessibilityAction(named:)`. The sizes of the selector do not scale with Dynamic Type: `selectorTitleWidth` 260, `selectorListWidth` 300, and `selectorListHeight` 480.
4. `_ = try?` in the `measure` blocks of `RenderingPerformanceTests` times an empty block after a throw and passes. `StressContent.swift:47`, `TemporaryFolder.swift:18`, and `NavigationTests.swift:43` drop errors too (SAFE-02).
5. Test comments and `#if targetEnvironment(simulator)` branches still name the simulator, which learned rule 1 bans.
6. Code without a caller in the app:
   - `ToolState.isTextEnabled`, `WALCheckpointer.completed`, `StrokeStore.blockListCount`, and `GlyphAtlas.placement(of:font:exponent:frame:)`.
   - `CanvasSessions.session(for:)`, `LiveStrokeHandoff.liveStrokeCount`, `HoverDotLayer.showsRing`, and `PencilKitStrokeConverter.pointSize(drawingWidth:)`.
   - The `navigate(to:camera:)` of the app, `AppComposition.sharedSnapshot` outside tests, and the empty `BackupFeature` namespace.
   - 17 public declarations of `Geometry`, `Model`, and `InkEngine` that only tests or their own package use.
7. DOC-01: about 30 internal declarations have no documentation comment. Most are in `RenderEngine`, `FrameRenderer`, `InkResources`, `WorkspaceList`, and `WorkspaceStrings`. The stored properties of `AppComposition` have none either.
8. FLOW-03: `SnapshotImport.swift:142` and `CardOpening.swift:46` keep the bound of a `while` loop in its body.
9. The allocation tests count only a pan frame. `FrameLoopProbe` does not count `RenderEngine.apply` or the commit handler, and no test counts a handoff, an eraser mask, or a redraw frame. No test shows that a `CanvasRenderer` or a `FrameRenderer` goes away.

## 4. Readiness for M10

The code is ready for M10: `make check` passes on the branch with 440 device tests (section 5), and the faults that a user can reach in M9 are fixed. M10 is not ready to start until the product owner decides items 1 to 4 of section 3.1. Item 1 decides how M10 acceptance 1 can pass at all. Item 2 decides how much work the contract pass of M10 is.

The M10 list of plan.md now holds the open items of section 3 of this review. The glyph quads of D-94 option (b) need a design first: the atlas reuses page indexes, so a buffer of one box cannot keep atlas positions without a table per exponent or a generation counter. Pages of different sizes need a texture array or an argument buffer for one draw per box. The fallback fonts need dense indexes, because the layout runs off the render thread (D-92). Items 4 and 6 of section 3.3 touch the same code and come first.
